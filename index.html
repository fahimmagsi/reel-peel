About Reel Peel

Reel Peel is a next-generation social media management suite built specifically for short-form video creators, influencers, and digital marketing teams. By connecting directly with TikTok for Developers APIs (Login Kit, Share Kit, Content Posting API), creators can upload video drafts, schedule reel distribution, review performance metrics, and maintain full control over their social footprint.

🎵

TikTok API Integration & Security

Reel Peel uses official, sandboxed OAuth 2.0 protocols to interact with TikTok APIs. We never store user passwords and adhere strictly to TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Partner Privacy Policies.

Key Platform Capabilities

  • Unified Content Scheduling: Plan video releases across multiple days and peak engagement hours.
  • Direct Publishing & Drafts: Seamlessly post or prepare drafts using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Creator Privacy First: User data is never sold, leased, or used for unauthorized profiling. Full on-demand data deletion is guaranteed.
  • Cross-Platform Support: Available across Web browsers, Desktop environments, Android, and iOS devices.
