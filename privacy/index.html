1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Reel Peel ("we," "our," "us," or "the Application"). Reel Peel is a social media management platform designed to help content creators, businesses, and individuals manage, organize, schedule, and publish video content across multiple social media platforms, including TikTok.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal data in compliance with international data privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the TikTok Developer Platform Terms of Service and Partner Privacy Policy.

By downloading, accessing, or using Reel Peel (via Web, Desktop, Android, or iOS), you agree to the collection and handling of your data as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, optimize, and secure Reel Peel's services:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address, display name, profile avatar, and account preferences.
  • User-Generated Content: Video files, captions, tags, scheduled publishing times, thumbnail images, and associated metadata that you upload or prepare for publishing.
  • Device and Technical Information: IP address, operating system, browser type, app version, crash reports, and system telemetry to troubleshoot issues and ensure service stability.
  • Third-Party Authentication Data: OAuth tokens, permission scopes, and user identifiers granted when you connect external accounts such as TikTok.

3. TikTok API Data & Compliance

🎵

Compliance with TikTok Developer Policies

Reel Peel accesses TikTok user data exclusively through official TikTok for Developers APIs (such as Login Kit, Share Kit, and Content Posting API) based strictly on explicit permissions and scopes granted by the user.

When you connect your TikTok account to Reel Peel, we access and process only the permissions you explicitly authorize:

  • Basic Profile Information: TikTok Open ID, display name, and avatar image to display your connected account within the Reel Peel dashboard.
  • Content Posting & Management: Uploading, scheduling, and publishing video files and caption metadata to your TikTok profile upon your direct command.
  • Tokens: Temporary OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens used to authenticate requests securely without storing your TikTok password.

Important: We do not sell your TikTok data, transfer it to data brokers, or use it for automated facial recognition, biometric profiling, or targeted external advertising.

4. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide core Reel Peel functionalities (video scheduling, multi-account publishing, workflow automation).
  • To authenticate your identity and maintain secure sessions.
  • To execute user-initiated publishing and upload actions to connected social media platforms.
  • To communicate crucial updates, security alerts, and customer support responses.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical bugs, fraudulent activities, or violations of our Terms of Service.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data.

We only share data under the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Social Media Platforms (TikTok / ByteDance): When you choose to publish a video, your video, caption, and tokens are transmitted to TikTok's official API servers to complete the upload request.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: Trusted cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., secure hosting, databases) that assist in delivering our services under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation to protect the rights, property, and safety of Reel Peel, our users, or the public.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

  • All network communications are encrypted via TLS/HTTPS (SSL).
  • OAuth tokens and credentials are securely encrypted at rest.
  • We retain personal information only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You can delete your data at any time.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your geographic location, you have statutory privacy rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke granted permissions or disconnect third-party accounts at any time.

8. User Data Deletion Instructions

🗑️

How to Request Immediate Data Deletion

Reel Peel provides simple, transparent methods for users to remove their information and revoke TikTok platform access.

You can delete your data through any of the following methods:

  1. Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to fahimmagsi@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Reel Peel". We will verify and permanently delete all your account records, access tokens, and uploaded media within 7 business days.
  2. Revoke TikTok Permissions: You can immediately disconnect Reel Peel from your TikTok account by navigating to:
    TikTok App → Profile → Settings and Privacy → Security & Permissions → Apps and Services → Reel Peel → Remove / Revoke Access.

9. Children's Privacy

Reel Peel is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at fahimmagsi@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information.
